# Audit of Archived `MyHomeSystemEmulator@4f93f44ee5ec` vs. Real BTicino Firmware & Hardware

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit compares the archived Qt4 C++ `OpenWebNet-HA/MyHomeSystemEmulator` (`4f93f44ee5ec7f89a3de9e040141755c847a5eda`) against:
1. Emulated production gateway binaries (`MH200/020102`, `MH200N/010108`, `F454/020051`, `MyHomeServer1/028206`, `F461/020011`) in `own-firmware-oracle`.
2. Physical bench captures from `F461` (`2026-10-15`, `2026-10-18`, `2026-10-19`) and `MH200` (`cap-mh200-*` Encyclopedia evidence bundles).

Because `MyHomeSystemEmulator` mixes LGPL-2.1+ (`btf422_dev.cpp`, `btscsbus.cpp`) and unlicensed files (`btf411dev.cpp`) and never modeled the SCS byte protocol, `oracle/plant/` in `own-firmware-oracle` is implemented **100% clean-room under Apache-2.0** from firmware disassembly and hardware captures.

## 2. Structural & Protocol Divergences in `MyHomeSystemEmulator`

| Subsystem | `MyHomeSystemEmulator@4f93f44ee5ec` Behavior | Real Gateway Firmware & Physical Hardware Behavior | Clean-Room `oracle/plant/` Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SCS Bus Layer (`btscsbus.cpp`)** | Pass-through of high-level `PlantMessage` key/value strings (`OpenMsg` ASCII); no `$03`/`$003` or `$17B8`/`$018D2` PIC framing or XOR checksums. | `bt_luci`, `coso`, `bt_termo`, `bt_energia`, and `driver_manager` exchange `$03`/`$04`/`$06` (2-digit) or `$003`/`$004`/`$006` (3-digit RPMsg) PIC frames with XOR checksums and require `$08\r`/`$00800\r` TX-ACK + `$17`/`$18` (`$017`/`$018`) inbound replies. | `oracle/plant/scs_codec.py` and `oracle/bus.py` implement full 2-digit and 3-digit PIC framing, TX-ACK (`$08\r`/`$00800\r`), and 25 ms UART frame pacing. |
| **ACK Generation Ownership** | Individual device plugins (`btf411dev.cpp`, `btf520dev.cpp`) emit `*#*1##` (`MSG_ACK`) directly in the OpenWebNet reply list. | Gateway daemons (`own_server`, `bt_luci`, `coso`, `bt_termo`) control `*#*1##`/`*#*0##` based on bus ACK and device reply timing; e.g., `F461` NACKs unpopulated `*1*1*08##` after `2.06 s` while ACKing `*#1*08*#1*150*0##` in `177 ms`. | `PlantResponder` emits only SCS bus telegrams; `OwnPlantEngine` applies per-gateway and per-frame-category ACK rules. |
| **WHO=1 Area 0 Addressing (`btf411dev.cpp`)** | `execCommand()` checks the area range (`1..9` / `00`) before the point range, routing 2-digit Area 0 points (`01`..`09`) into the area branch. | `F461`, `F454`, `MH200N`, and `MyHomeServer1` address `01`..`09` as Area 0 point-to-point relays (`b8 01..09`), while `MH200/020102` rejects 2-digit Area 0 commands (`*1*1*02##` $\to$ NACK) and requires 1-digit `*1*1*2##`. | `where_to_bus_addr()` and `LightDevice` distinguish Area 0 P2P (`01`..`09`, `0001`..`0015`) from Area broadcasts (`00`, `1`..`9`). |
| **WHO=1 Group `#G` & `DIMENSION 15`** | Group command switches member relays and emits per-point frames only; no `DIMENSION 15` group aggregate status frame is emitted. | `f461/bt_luci` (`23scs_answer_group_status` at `0x443d0`) translates `B5 <grp> D0 <00\|01>` into `*#1*#<G>*15*<1\|0>##` whenever a group transitions between all-OFF and any-ON. General `*1*1*0##` emits `*1*1*0##` + `*#1*#1*15*1##` without per-point frames. | `PlantResponder` and `OwnPlantEngine` emit `B5 <grp> D0 <00\|01>` / `*#1*#<G>*15*<1\|0>##` on group aggregate state transitions. |
| **WHO=1 Dimmers (`F414`, `F418U2`)** | No dimmer plugin exists in `MyHomeSystemEmulator`; `F411` only supports binary `WHAT=0, 1`. | Physical `F414` and `F418U2` dimmers map `WHAT=2..10` to non-linear `DIMENSION 1` hardware curves (`100..200`, `speed=2`) and emit `*#1*<W>*1*<lvl>*5##` when `DIM #1` is written with `speed=0`. | `DimmerDevice` implements the exact `F414` and `F418U2` level lookup tables from `cap-mh200-f414-dimmer-20260926` and `cap-mh200-f418u2-dimmer-20260927`. |
| **WHO=2 Shutters (`F411/2`) & `WHO=14` Lock** | `btf411dev.cpp` models `F411/2` as a 2-channel light relay; no `WHO=2` shutter state machine, travel timer, or `WHO=14` actuator lock exists. | `coso` sends pre-move `STOP` (`12 0A`) before `UP`/`DOWN` (`12 08`/`09`), producing `['*2*1000#0*05##', '*2*0*05##', '*2*1000#1*05##', '*2*1*05##']` and end-of-travel `*2*0*05##` after `duration_ms`. `WHO=14` (`*14*0*W##`) locks relays and shutters (`11 00` $\to$ `B8 W 14 01`). | `CoverDevice` and `LightDevice` implement `coso` pre-move `STOP`, travel timers via `tick(now_ms)`, and `WHO=14` lock freeze. |
| **F422 Sub-Bus Bridge (`btf422_dev.cpp`)** | Forwards all sub-bus commands and area commands indiscriminately once unwrapped. | Physical `F422` (`cap-mh200-f422-scope-20260923`) answers area status `*#2*1#4#02##` per-point (`11#4#02..13#4#02`), ACKs area command `*2*1*1#4#02##` with **no movement**, executes P2P `*2*1*11#4#02##`, and isolates main-bus broadcasts. | `scs_codec.py`, `PlantResponder`, and `OwnPlantEngine` enforce exact `cap-mh200-f422-scope-20260923` sub-bus routing and isolation. |
| **WHO=4 Climate (`KW4691`)** | No `WHO=4` thermostat plugin exists in `MyHomeSystemEmulator`. | `bt_termo` (`0xad60..0xae70`) runs a 4-step SCS query (`30 00`, `30 12`, `30 0E`, `30 0D`) on `*#4*2##` and emits the 5-frame burst `['*#4*2*0*T##', '*#4*2*12*SP*3##', '*4*M*2##', '*#4*2*14*SP*3##', '*#4*2*13*00##']`. On `F461/020011`, `*#4*2*22##` crashes `bt_termo`. | `ThermoZoneDevice` answers all 4 `bt_termo` SCS steps and models standalone `KW4691` heating/cooling/protection/off modes and the `F461` `DIM 22` crash. |
| **WHO=18 Energy (`btf520dev.cpp`)** | `genmsgworker.cpp` appends a trailing duplicate frame on `DIM 511..514` queries and uses wall-clock time. | `bt_energia` translates `D1 <A1..A9> 02 33 ...` queries into single `D2`/`D5` replies (`DIM 113, 51..54, 71, 72, 73#H, 1200`). | `EnergyMeterDevice` and `EnergyActuatorDevice` use a deterministic fixed epoch and emit single well-formed SCS `D2`/`D5` replies. |
| **Repository Hygiene** | Contains `bt_own_GTW_PGIN/resources/mycertkey.pem` (a committed RSA private key) and author-named `.plnt` files. | Never imported into `own-firmware-oracle`. | Clean-room YAML fixtures (`f461-bench.yaml`, `mh200-bench.yaml`) and clean-room `load_plnt_xml()` parser. |
